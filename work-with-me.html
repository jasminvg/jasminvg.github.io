How projects are scoped

Project sizes

Projects are scoped and priced by deliverable. Each one ends with materials your organization owns and can keep using.

01

Seed

One focused deliverable, such as a research brief, a facilitation guide, or a bilingual glossary.

About 2–4 weeks

02

Root System

A full project in one service line, such as an oral history set, an engagement plan with a workshop, or a public report.

About 1–3 months

03

Ecosystem

Multi-line work, such as a place-based study, a community archive, or a landscape study for funders.

About 3–9 months

How we work

Plant, tend, harvest

  1. Plant. We start with a simple question: what plant are you? Extractive, neutral, or growing in reciprocity with your neighbors?
  2. Tend. We co-design the scope, the timeline, and what you'll own at the end.
  3. Harvest. You walk away with tools, archives, and stories that are yours to keep using.
